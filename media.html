How It Works

  1. Consultation – We discuss goals, timelines, and where your content will live (web, social, email, press).
  2. Discovery & Interview – You complete a short questionnaire and/or recorded conversation so we can capture your story and voice.
  3. Drafts & Collaboration – We create written content, pair it with visuals, and refine everything based on your feedback.
  4. Final Delivery – You receive web-ready, share-ready files you can plug directly into your site, platforms, and campaigns.

Client Example: Pittsburgh Prestige Remodeling

How a carpentry and remodeling studio can turn one Brand Media Package into website copy, social content, and long-term marketing assets.

Brand Story (Excerpt)

“Pittsburgh Prestige Remodeling was built on a simple belief: craftsmanship should last a lifetime. Founded by lead carpenter Michael Allen, Prestige has become one of Pittsburgh’s most trusted names in custom kitchens, luxury built‑ins, and full‑home renovations. With a focus on precision, integrity, and clean modern design, every project is treated like a legacy piece — made to elevate the homes and lives of the people who live in them.”

Event Recap (Showcase)

“To celebrate the completion of their South Hills whole‑home remodel, Pittsburgh Prestige Remodeling opened the doors to partners, clients, and community members for an exclusive walkthrough. Highlights included custom white‑oak cabinetry, floor‑to‑ceiling stonework, and a live demonstration of their new integrated lighting installations. Guests described the work as ‘museum‑level craftsmanship’ and ‘some of the cleanest carpentry in the city.’ Photos from the showcase were used for marketing, PR outreach, and the client’s website relaunch.”

Social Caption Pack (Sample)

  • “Every clean line has a story. Every detail has a purpose. This is craftsmanship built to live with you, not just impress you.”
  • “South Hills project reveal — modern, warm, and intentionally designed. Which room should we highlight next?”
  • “From framing to finish work, our team treats every home like a long‑term investment in quality.”

How Pittsburgh Prestige Can Implement This Package

Pittsburgh Prestige Remodeling can plug this content directly into their business:

  • Website: Use the brand story and recap on the homepage, About page, and project pages to build trust with homeowners.
  • Social Media: Pair the caption pack with project photos for a month of consistent, on-brand posts.
  • Proposals & Estimates: Drop story excerpts and images into proposal PDFs to communicate value beyond a line-item quote.
  • PR & Community Outreach: Adapt the recap for neighborhood groups, renovation blogs, and local media.
  • Email Follow-Up: Turn the recap and story into a newsletter for past clients and referral partners.
