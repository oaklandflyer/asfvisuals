Founder

Andrew Coutinho

Andrew Coutinho is the founder of ASF Visuals LLC — a Pittsburgh-based visual media studio that merges creative storytelling with technical precision. Every project is built around one question: does this make your business grow?

His work has been featured by clients including the University of Pittsburgh, regional real estate firms, and local contractors. He hand-built this very website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — the same skills he brings to every web project.

Andrew holds a dual degree in Economics and Africana Studies from the University of Pittsburgh, and completed the Fulbright-Hays Group Projects Abroad Program in Tanzania — deepening his perspective on community, development, and how culture shapes creativity.

Beyond photography, he serves as Vice Curator of the WEF Global Shapers Pittsburgh Hub and founded the National Collegiate Bodybuilding Organization (NCBO), connecting 10+ universities. The same skills — project management, community building, strategic execution — inform every client engagement.
