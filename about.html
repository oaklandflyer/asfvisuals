About Me

Andrew Coutinho — founder of ASF Visuals LLC, a drone and landscape photography company that merges creative storytelling with technical precision. I specialize in capturing dynamic visuals that elevate brands, spaces, and experiences.

My work has been featured by clients like the University of Pittsburgh and regional real estate firms, and I custom-built this very website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to bring my portfolio to life. ASF Visuals reflects my belief that design, imagery, and authenticity should work together to tell a clear visual story.

I earned a dual degree in Economics and Africana Studies from the University of Pittsburgh, where I also served as a Fulbright-Hays Scholar in Tanzania. That experience deepened my perspective on community, development, and how culture shapes creativity — all of which inform the way I approach my work today.

Beyond photography, I founded and lead the University of Pittsburgh Bodybuilding Club, building it into a connected network of athletes across multiple universities. Leading that organization strengthened my skills in project management, collaboration, and brand growth — the same principles I bring to every client project.

I’m continuing to expand ASF Visuals through fine art prints, branding visuals, and aerial storytelling that connect people to place. If you’re looking for a creative partner who combines vision, structure, and execution — let’s connect.
